Backup & import

In the menu that opens, tap Save to Files, choose a folder in iCloud Drive (for example “Breathing backups”), then tap Save. The file also opens in Numbers or Excel and can be emailed to your vet. Removing this app from your Home Screen can erase its data, so back up now and then.

Import a backup from this app, or a spreadsheet saved as CSV. You’ll see what will be added before anything changes; readings you already have are skipped.
